Picture packager

Assign each picture to a vehicle or screen, and get a folder that drops straight into the Proof Engine repository. It runs only in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Use only pictures you have permission to use. The Maruti Suzuki website's terms do not allow copying its logos or photographs without written consent. Good sources are an organiser media kit, a written permission from Maruti Suzuki, or your own photographs. This tool does not download anything from the web.

1. Add your pictures

Choose as many JPG, PNG or WebP files as you like. The tool guesses the vehicle from the file name (for example brezza-side.png or Grand Vitara front.jpg). You can change any guess in step 2.

Drag pictures here, or choose them from your computer.

2. Say what each picture is

Pick the vehicle and view for every file. Each place in the app can take one picture. Files marked "Skip" are left out.
